DESKMATE OS 1.6 · BLUETOOTH SETUP

Set DeskMate's Wi-Fi

This browser can't use Bluetooth

Use Chrome or Edge on a Mac, Windows PC or Android phone. On an iPhone, join DeskMate's own Wi-Fi instead (Settings → Phone setup on DeskMate).

  1. On DeskMate, Bluetooth setup is open when it has no Wi-Fi, while phone setup is open, or after Settings → Bluetooth → Wi-Fi setup.
  2. Click Connect, pick DeskMate, and when your computer asks, type the 6-digit code DeskMate shows.
  3. Choose your network, enter its password, and click Send. DeskMate keeps up to 4 networks.
